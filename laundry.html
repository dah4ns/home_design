Laundry Wall Elevation (0.08 Pralnia)

2D Furniture Elevations for the North Wall (Width: 3,970 mm, Height: 2,800 mm). Compare the Original Plan with the Senior Interior Designer Alternative Approach.

✨ Senior Interior Designer Drying-First Architecture — 5 Integrated Drying Systems (11.0+ m Air-Drying Capacity)

In the original plan, air-drying was limited to a single short 800 mm rod—leaving nowhere to dry long dresses/trousers, flat wool sweaters, dripping hand-wash items, or small garments without cluttering the floor with freestanding racks. While preserving the exact 3,970 × 2,800 mm wall envelope and horizontal base rhythm (670 + 450 + 650 + 800 + 600 + 800 mm), this design transforms the wall into a 5-Zone Multi-Mode Drying Ecosystem:

1. Left 670 mm Open Drying Bay — 4× Półki Ażurowe (co 45 cm)
Activates the full 670 × 2,400 mm left alcove with 4 rows of breathable openwork drying shelves (4× półki ażurowe) spaced 45 cm (450 mm) apart (Z = 600, 1,050, 1,500, 1,950 mm) for generous air circulation, flat-drying knitwear, and airy linen storage.
2. 1,360 mm Shirt Rod + Over-Sink Drip-Dry Zone
Removing the x=257 partition extends the hanging rod across the entire 1,400 mm niche (Z = 1,680 mm)—including a 600 mm wet drip-dry section directly over the 480 mm sink so hand-washed clothes drip straight into the drain.
3. Dual Pull-Out Flat Mesh Sweater Racks (800 mm)
Below the hanging shirts (Z = 1,020..1,150 mm), two pull-out breathable mesh frames (Siatki na swetry) slide out for drying cashmere & wool flat without stretching, and tuck flush when not in use.
4. 2 × 200 mm Pull-Out Vertical Drying Cabinets (Right)
Turns the two narrow 200 mm right-side slots (x=317..357, Z = 150..1,800 mm) into 5-tier pull-out drying racks (Cargo Suszarka I & II = 6.0 m drying line) with ventilated slatted-oak fronts for socks, t-shirts & underwear.
5. Stacked Dryer + Z = 900 mm Pull-Out Basket Shelf + Aligned Grid
Includes a 60 mm pull-out oak basket shelf between Washer & Dryer, a continuous 1,400 mm oak folding worktop, and 100% aligned 800 + 600 + 800 mm upper cabinets.
