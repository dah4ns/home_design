Multi-View Orientation: Why Behind the Stairs is NORTH

In this house's global coordinate system, the origin X = 0 mm is at the Eastmost corner of the North Facade, and positive X grows Westward (up to 20,590 mm).

When viewing the Stairs (Schody) from inside the house, you are looking North towards the exterior North Wall. Therefore:

  • View Left Edge: Positioned exactly at X = 18,910 mm in global building coordinates.
  • Lower Level Sequence (Project): 150 mm wall + 900 mm door D1 (leaf 800×2050, hole 900×2100) + 463 mm wall + 900 mm door D2 (leaf 800×2050, hole 900×2100) + 56 mm wall + 80 mm brick wall = 2,549 mm to the start of the stairs!
  • Stair Turn & Flight Along North Wall: Exactly 4 first stairs contribute to the turn: Stair 1 is a straight approach step; Stairs 2, 3, and 4 build the 90° winder turn in the 100×100 cm corner. All subsequent stairs (Stairs 5 to 19) go straight along the North Wall with 17.5 cm rise × 28 cm tread depth to finished +3,33 m.
  • Upper Level Sequence: 1,575 mm recessed background wall + 180 mm closer wall + 900 mm door opening (leaf 800×2050, hole 900×2100) + 730 mm closer wall + 80 mm brick wall (replaces vertical wall above stairs) = 3,465 mm total to stairwell opening!
  • Ceiling & Headroom Soffit Corner: Suspended ceiling (16 cm cavity down to +2,80 m) is removed directly above the stairs (X = 2,549 to 3,465 mm), exposing the structural concrete slab soffit at +2,96 m for 226 cm vertical clearance and 209 cm diagonal clearance.
  • Window O11 Note: Window O11 is located on the North Facade exterior wall behind this corridor wall (separated by an intervening wall not visible in this interior section view), and is therefore not rendered on this interior corridor elevation.
  • Upper Right Floor & Brick Wall: To the right of the stair landing at X = 7,189 mm, the upper finished floor extends for exactly 940 mm (to X = 8,129 mm), terminating at an 80 mm exposed clay brick wall (to X = 8,209 mm) preceding Room 1.01 Sypialnia.
