✨ Interior Designer "Outside-the-Box" Proposal: 80.5 cm Housekeeping & Vacuum Charging Dock + Zero-Dead-Corner Pantry

Why put a 50 cm drawer unit on the North Wall if it blocks 45 cm of the East Wall? By dedicating the entire 805 mm (80.5 cm) between the Samsung Freezer and the East cabinets to a 190 cm tall Housekeeping & Charging Closet (Szafa gospodarcza: Drabina + Odkurzacz pionowy + Mop)—and opening the 450 × 450 mm space under the North-East countertop sideways into the closet—we eliminate the dead corner completely! You get an 80.5 cm wide utility bay (125.5 cm wide at floor level including the hidden corner bucket garage!) plus an unbroken 200 cm East quartz worktop and 3 × 500 mm pull-out crate towers.

1. 805 mm Housekeeping & Vacuum Dock
Aligned with the 190 cm Freezer: fits a tall 4-step ladder (20 cm) + wall-hung cordless vacuum on 230V charger (32 cm) + wall-clipped mop & broom (28 cm).
2. "Side-Access" Corner Bucket Garage (45×45 cm)
Instead of a dead corner, the 450×450×880 mm cube under the corner of the East worktop opens sideways into the utility closet to hide the full-size mop bucket!
3. Continuous Overhead Crown (Z = 1950..2800)
A unified 850 mm tall band of deep storage cabinets runs across the entire North wall above both the Freezer and the 190 cm Utility Closet.
4. 200 cm East Worktop + Corner Appliance Garage
Above Z = 900 mm, the East quartz worktop runs all the way into the North-East corner (45×45 cm appliance garage) above 3 × 500 mm drawer towers.
