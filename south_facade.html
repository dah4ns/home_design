Eastward Coordinate Transformation & Vertical Alignments

Unlike the North Facade (which looks South and has direction: "west"), the South Facade looks North from the garden and has direction: "east".

🧭 1. View Definition (south_facade)

• Position on Map (Left Edge): 21,000 mm (West Corner)
• Direction: "east" (Left-to-right moves West → East)
• Overall Length: 21,000 mm (East Corner at 0 mm)
• Screen Formula: localX = 21,000 - (win.x + win.width)

⚡ 2. Directional Shift Behavior

• Increasing global X by +100 mm moves a window Westward, which shifts it to the LEFT on the South Facade.
• Decreasing global X by -100 mm moves a window Eastward, which shifts it to the RIGHT on the South Facade.

🎯 3. Vertical Architectural Alignments

• At 6,000 mm from West edge: Right edge of ground window SO5 aligns with left edge of upper window SO2.
• At 14,500 mm from West edge: Left edge of ground HST door SO7 aligns with left edge of upper window SO4.
• At 20,800 mm from West edge: Right edges of SO7 and SO4 share a 200 mm East corner pier.
